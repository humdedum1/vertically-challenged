## Terminal colour schemes

`src/themes/terminal/*.json` are generated by `scripts/terminal-themes.mjs` from terminal
colour schemes; the generator derives UI roles from them and adjusts colours for contrast.

- Tabby (https://github.com/Eugeny/tabby): the Tabby Default and Tabby Default Light schemes,
  MIT License, Copyright (c) 2017 Eugene Pankov.
